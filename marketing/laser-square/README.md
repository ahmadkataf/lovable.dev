Second promo for منجرة الإبداع, same layout as `../carpentry-square`. It covers laser cutting and engraving on acrylic, wood and MDF.

The palette is dark charcoal, laser orange and plywood beige.

- `design.html` holds the source.
- `laser-square.png` is the exported image at 2160×2160.
- `wa-qr.svg` is the WhatsApp QR code. It opens a chat with https://wa.me/963996489504.
