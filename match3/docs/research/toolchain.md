## Summary

Corrections:
- The machine-state fact was stale: platforms;android-37.0 r2 is now installed in /opt/android-sdk, plus an emulator and system images.
- Two source URLs were wrong: AGP 9.5 compatibility is on agp-9-5-0-release-notes, and the KGP override recipe is in the AGP 9.0 release notes.
