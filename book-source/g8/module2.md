#### Grammar point 2: Reported Speech

Explanation/model (verbatim):

**Reported Speech**
**It's a bad phone line and we could only hear a few sentences of Jerry's phone call.**
"I'm sitting in a train station in New Delhi but I don't have a lot of time. I don't have a laptop here so I can't check my email every day. I'll call you when I find a room with Wi-Fi. I've tried so many new kinds of food here in India. I've eaten spicy food and it is delicious. I'm going to travel to Egypt next week to see the Pyramids. I miss you!"

**Reporting what Jerry said.**
Jerry said that he was sitting in a train station in New Delhi but he didn't have a lot of time. He told his friend that he didn't have a laptop there so he couldn't check his email every day. He would call his friend when he found a room with Wi-Fi. He said that he had tried so many new kinds of food there in India. He had eaten spicy food and it was delicious. He added that he was going to travel to Egypt the week after to see the Pyramids. At the end of the call he told his friend that he missed him.

Rule summary (derived from the model): when reporting, tenses shift back (present simple → past simple; present continuous → past continuous; present perfect → past perfect; will → would; can → could; am/is going to → was going to), pronouns change (I → he, you → his friend), and time/place words change (here → there, next week → the week after).

**- Select the correct words between brackets.**
1. *"I've forgotten to bring my lunch with me".*
   Jim said that he had forgotten to bring (his, my) lunch with him. → **his**
2. *" We will be home soon".*
   My parents told me that they (would be, will be) home soon. → **would be**
3. *"I'll clean the car tomorrow".*
   (He, I) told his father that he would clean the car the following day. → **He**
4. *"I have finished reading the story".*
   Gary said that he (has finished, had finished). → **had finished**
5. *"I'm preparing for the exam".*
   My sister said that she (is preparing, was preparing) for the exam. → **was preparing**

**A- Complete the sentences from the text above.**
1. The World Tour Agency .............many people ................the Iguazu Falls. → **said that** ... **had visited**

**B- Select the correct form.**
1. Tom: "I was tired".

2. Sarah: "I went home early".

### Climate & Weather (full text, verbatim)

The difference between weather and climate is a measure of time. Weather is what the conditions of the atmosphere are over a short period of time, and climate is how the atmosphere changes over relatively long periods of time.

Sometimes the climate differs from one area to another in the same country, for example, Peru has a variety of climate types to choose where to go. It has the blue sky and sunshine of the tropical northern coast, the humid hot Amazon rainforest and the cold weather of the Andes, there is a climate to suit everyone's taste in Peru. On the other hand, some countries have moderate, cold or hot climate all over the year. For example, Portugal enjoys a warm temperate climate with wet winters and dry summers.

**B - Decide if the statements are True or False.**

2. Peru has the same climate in all its areas. → **False** (it has a variety of climate types)
3. The climate in Portugal is warm. → **True** (a warm temperate climate)

**C - Write four sentences describing the climate of Syria in the four seasons.**

**D - Answer the following question with "Yes" or "No".**
If the day before the day after the day before today was hotter than the day after the day before today, was the day before today hotter than today?
