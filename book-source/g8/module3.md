### Reading (page 35)
Photo: a sad girl in an orange sweater looking at her blue phone; two girls behind her look at a phone and whisper (blurred).

**Read the article and do the tasks below.**

#### Beware!

**What is bullying?**
Bullying is a repeated, harsh, aggressive behaviour that can be physical, verbal,social or online.You may live in a constant fear of where and when the bullies will strike next what they will do, and how far they will go.
Cyber bullying can occur any where. It happens even at home, via smart phones, emails texts, and social media, 24 hours a day. Cyber bullies use digital technology to annoy, threaten, or humiliate others, unlike traditional bullying which used to occur in streets, school yards or restaurants. Cyber bullying doesn't require face-to-face contact.
Bullying can follow you anywhere so that.no place, not even home, makes you feel safe.
It's important to remember that you're not alone. There are plenty of people who can help you overcome the problem and retain your dignity.

**How to deal with a bully?**
There are many ways to deal with a bully. Firstly, you have to keep in mind that there is no simple solution to bullying or cyber bullying. You don't have to blame yourself.
It is not your fault, no matter what a bully says or does. The bully is the person with the problem, not you. Secondly, try to find support and encouragement. Talking to a parent, teacher, counselor, or other trusted adults doesn't mean that you're weak.
Thirdly, take a break from your smart phone, computer, tablet, and video games.
Meet new people and connect with real friends who don't participate in any kind of bullying and who share the same values and interests with you.

Amazon's business made online shopping so easy and **convenient** that customers won't think twice to use it. "Buy now with one click".
