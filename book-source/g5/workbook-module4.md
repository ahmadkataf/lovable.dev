Bees make honey. They live in hives. A hive has only one queen, many 
worker bees, drones and male bees. It usually has around 50,000 bees. 
Worker bees go from one flower to another, collecting nectar. When they 
sip the nectar, they store it in their bodies in order to make honey when 
they return to the hive. In one trip, a bee visits 50 to 100 flowers. Bees 
have 5 eyes and 6 legs. It takes a great effort and hard work from bees 
to make the honey we enjoy.
