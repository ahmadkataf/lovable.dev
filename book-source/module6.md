Full text (verbatim; words in **bold** are bold/key words in the book):

Telekinesis is simply the ability to move an object in some manner without coming into physical contact with it.

In 1960, a Russian housewife videoed herself moving the tableware by just looking at it. She could also break an egg and separate the white from the **yolk** without touching it. She accidently discovered her ability to do so.

### Grammar (page 83)

Grammar point: **Articles** (a / an / the / no article)

The book gives no rule box on this page (the page decoration repeats the word "Grammar" with leaf shapes). Rule (supplied for the app): use **a** before a consonant sound and **an** before a vowel sound when talking about one of many, unspecified things (first mention); use **the** for a specific thing already known or mentioned, or when there is only one (the U.S.A, the office); use **no article (x)** with plural or uncountable nouns in general statements and with days of the week, meals, etc.

3- ..........scientists said that emotional states can affect the external environment. → **x (no article)** — the text says "Scientists also said..."
4- Clerk discovered that he could move .......... cups on the shelves and make them fly. → **the**

**B- Select the correct article.**

### Grammar (page 89)

Grammar point: **Passive voice**

The book gives no rule box; rule (supplied for the app): the passive is formed with the verb **to be** (in the right tense) + the **past participle**: *is/are + p.p.* (present), *was/were + p.p.* (past), *can be / cannot be + p.p.*, *to be + p.p.* after another verb. Use *by* to say who does the action.

**A - Complete the following sentences from the text.**
1- This phenomenon .......... UFO. → **is called**
2- The term UFO .......... to talk about any observations of alien spaceships. → **is used**
3- Science-fiction movies started to .......... so we can say that everyone of us has seen a spaceship either in real life or in a movie. → **be made**
4- Some secret experiments .......... by governments. → **are done**
5- It is called unidentified because it.......... . → **cannot be immediately explained**

**B - Put the correct form of verb (to be) to make passive sentences.**

4- English .......... spoken all over the world. → **is**
