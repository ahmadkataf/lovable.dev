**Title: Faces**

Full text (verbatim):

My sister, Doris, is a good-looking person.
She has green eyes, fair straight hair and a round face.
She is taller than me. She is very elegant and generally dressed in black skirts,red shirts,blue **cardigans** and black shoes.She is a smart woman.

She's got straight hair with a **fringe** and her bright blue eyes are the first thing you notice about her. She's got a lovely smile.

She used to like wearing flowery dresses,but now she thinks that they are more "girlish." Most of the time,she just puts on jeans and a T-shirt. If you saw her in the street,you would probably think she was two or three years older.

**Title: The Arrogant Rose**

Full text (verbatim):

Once upon a time, in a desert far away, there lived a rose who was so proud of her beautiful looks.
She believed that she was the nicest in the desert.
Her only complaint was growing next to an ugly cactus.

Every day, the beautiful rose would insult and mock the cactus, while the cactus remainded quiet and say not a word. All the other plants nearby tried to make the rose stop behaving foolishly, but she was arrogant and moved back and forth.
One scorching summer, the desert became dry, and there was no water left for the plants.

The rose quickly began to wilt. Her beautiful petals dried up, losing their bright color.
Looking at the cactus, she saw a sparrow that was dipping his beak into the cactus to drink some water. Though the rose was ashamed, she asked the cactus if she could have some water.
The kind cactus immediately agreed to help them both through the tough summer, as friends.
